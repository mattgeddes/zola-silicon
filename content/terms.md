This is sample content shipped with a website template. Replace it before you
publish anything.

## What belongs in real terms

- **Who may use the site** and any eligibility requirements.
- **Accounts.** Responsibilities, and what happens when they are abandoned.
- **Acceptable use.** The behaviour that gets an account suspended.
- **Intellectual property.** Who owns what, including what users post.
- **Warranties and liability.** What is promised, and what is not.
- **Changes.** How you are told about them, and how to object.
- **Governing law** and where disputes are heard.

## A note on drafting

Terms written to be unread are not worth the ink. Short sections that a person
can follow in five minutes do more for trust than six pages of defensively
worded text, and they are easier to update when the product changes.
