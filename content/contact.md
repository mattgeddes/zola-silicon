Tell us what you are trying to do rather than what you think you need. If the
answer is that you do not need us, we will say that, and it will not lead to a
follow-up email.

## What happens next

1. **We read it.** A person, not a queue. Usually within a working day.
2. **A short call.** Thirty minutes, with an engineer who will be on the
   implementation if we end up working together.
3. **A straight answer.** A trial, a scoping call, or a referral to someone
   better placed.

We do not run a demo sequence, and we do not pass your details to anyone.
