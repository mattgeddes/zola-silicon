Between "we cannot afford a head of platform" and "we cannot afford the
incidents", there is a useful middle. This is it.

## What it is

One named senior engineer, a few days a month, who knows your system. Not a
rotating pool and not a sales engineer — the same person, who has read your
code and remembers last quarter's decision.

## What they do

- Architecture review on anything that is expensive to change.
- Hiring input: writing the brief, reviewing the loop, and being honest when
  the answer is not to hire yet.
- Incident command when something breaks badly, and a review afterwards.
- The judgement calls. Not every decision is a technical one, and most of them
  do not need a full-time director to make well.

## What it is not

It is not outsourced management. We do not assign people to your team or
answer to your stakeholders on your behalf.

{% callout(type="tip") %}
Start with a single month. If the relationship is not working, that is a
thirty-day lesson rather than a twelve-month one.
