For teams who have outgrown `make deploy` and have not yet outgrown managing it
by hand. We build the platform, you keep it.

## What is in scope

- **Environments.** Reproducible, provisioned on demand, seeded from a template.
- **A deploy path.** Preview, staging and production, with the checks you
  actually want and none of the ones you do not.
- **Secrets handling.** Nothing in the repository, nothing in a shell history.
- **Runbooks.** Written during the work, not afterwards by someone else.

## What is not

We do not build a platform to be resold. If the natural outcome is a service
you will maintain, that is the outcome we aim for.

{% callout(type="tip") %}
The most useful thing we do in week one is delete infrastructure. Most teams
arrive with more automation than they need, running on platforms they have
outgrown.
{% end %}

## Working together

One weekly call, async by default, and a shared repository you own from day
one. We commit to the work in the same repository you commit to your product,
which makes the usual agency awkwardness impossible.
