Two weeks of looking at how your system actually fails, followed by a document
your team can act on without us in the room.

## What we look at

- Incident history over the last two quarters. What actually woke someone up.
- Alerting. Which rules have fired, which have never fired, and which fire for
  reasons nobody can explain.
- Deployment risk. Blast radius, rollback time, and what happens if the deploy
  is fine but the migration is not.
- SLOs, if you have them. If you do not, we explain what they would buy you
  before suggesting them.

## What you get

A written report, ranked by effort against impact, with a specific
recommendation for each finding. No dependency on us to implement it, and no
obligation to.

{% callout(type="note") %}
A review is a snapshot. Twelve months later the top finding is usually a
different one, which is the argument for treating this as a habit rather than
a project.
