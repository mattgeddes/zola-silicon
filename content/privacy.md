This is sample content shipped with a website template. It is here to show what
a legal page looks like, not to describe a real policy. **Replace it before you
publish anything.**

## What a real policy needs to cover

- **What is collected.** Analytics, form submissions, cookies.
- **Why.** The lawful basis for each, in plain language.
- **How long it is kept,** and what happens when the period ends.
- **Who else sees it.** List processors and third parties by name.
- **How to exercise rights.** Access, correction, deletion, portability,
  objection, and the supervisory authority to complain to.

## The template itself

The sample site ships a single small script, `static/js/main.js`. It stores one
value in your browser's local storage — your light or dark theme preference —
and sends nothing anywhere. There are no fonts, trackers or third-party
requests loaded from other origins.

## The demo newsletter form

The subscribe button on the home page and blog index is not connected to
anything. It does not transmit your address, and the script that handles it
tells you as much when you submit. Replace it with a real provider, or delete
it, before launch.
