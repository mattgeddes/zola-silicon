## Provenance

Every path in those files was authored specifically for this template. Nothing
was traced, copied or scraped from an icon set, a stock library or another
project, which is why there is no attribution to give.

The sample company ("Kestrel"), its product names, the customer logos and all
body copy are fictional, written for this template. They carry the same CC0
dedication, but you should replace them with your own material anyway — a
template that ships a real business is not much of a template.

## Third-party assets

There are none. The site loads no external fonts, icon CDNs, trackers or
images; the only network requests it makes are for its own files.
